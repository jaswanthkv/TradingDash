Starting capital: ₹10,00,000 (paper). Benchmark: Nifty Microcap 250 (26,032 on inception date).

## Approach

Built a 17-name starting basket from the top of the microcap band (SEBI/AMFI ranks 501–619
of ~750), favoring the larger/more liquid end of the Nifty Microcap 250 universe for the
first cycle, and diversifying across ~13 sectors so no single theme (defense, IT, NBFC,
chemicals, healthcare, etc.) dominates. Roughly equal-weighted (~5.5% NAV each) rather than
conviction-weighted, since there isn't yet a performance track record to justify concentration.

## Key risks flagged at entry

- **ASTRAMICRO (91x P/E) and SYRMA (74x P/E) and VIJAYA (80x P/E)** are priced for
  perfection — kept at standard weight, not oversized, specifically because of this.
- **ROUTE** posted an EPS miss this quarter (-31.9% vs estimate) — included at standard
  size as a contrarian/valuation call (13.4x P/E), not conviction-sized; first candidate
  for review if next quarter also disappoints.
- Universe is sourced from SEBI/AMFI Jul–Dec 2024 average market-cap ranks 501–750, which
  may have drifted slightly from NSE's live Nifty Microcap 250 reconstitution — flagged as
  a known data-freshness limitation, not a stock-picking one.
- Microcap liquidity: position sizes were kept small (~5.5% each, well under the 10% cap)
  partly because several of these names (ESABINDIA, ASTRAMICRO) have thin daily volumes —
  a real fund would need to size entries around average daily traded value more carefully
  than this v1 does.
